Who I am.

Hi, I'm Shourya Singh, a second-year B.Tech Computer Science Engineering student with a strong interest in Artificial Intelligence, modern software development, and hands-on hardware.

I enjoy building things that combine creativity with problem-solving — whether that's an AI-powered web app, a robot that has to survive an arena, or a photograph that captures a moment worth remembering. Every project teaches me something new, and I believe the best way to learn is by building.

Currently I'm focused on AI-powered applications, React, backend development, robotics projects, and photography — covering college events as part of my campus photography team.
